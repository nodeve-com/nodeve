foxess states frequency once per port, not once per leg.

The `*` block paired voltage with frequency because both gate on `grid-region`, not because both vary per leg. Three legs cannot hold different frequencies, so expanding the template minted nine rows asserting what the hardware cannot do. Frequency moves to `_` on the `out` and `eps` ports.

Nothing referenced the per-leg rows and no measurement ever attached to them, so this drops 6 specification rows and changes no sensor. Every frequency interval in the catalog now sits at `_`.
